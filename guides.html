before you start

orbit runs on windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) with rust from steam. check the status board first: if rust just patched, it says "updating" until it's safe.

check status before every session. if it isn't green, wait.
01

get your key

  1. pick a key length in the shop and check out.
  2. the key appears in my keys straight away, with a copy button.
02

download the loader

  1. open my keys and press download loader next to your key.
  2. save it somewhere you'll find it, for example a folder on your desktop.
  3. always download it again from my keys after an update. don't use copies from anywhere else.
03

activate your key

  1. open the loader and sign in with your chudcheats account.
  2. paste your key and press activate.
  3. the key locks to this pc (hwid). you can reset it from my keys if you change pc.
one key, one pc. 7-day keys include 1 hwid reset and 30-day keys include 2.
04

launch and open the menu

  1. start rust and wait for the main menu.
  2. press launch orbit in the loader. it shows "running" when it's attached.
  3. in game, press insert to open or close the menu.
05

configs

three configs ship with the loader: legit (the default), rage and streamer, which turns on the stream-proof overlay. switch between them in the loader or the menu, and your changes save per config.

06

troubleshooting

  1. loader says "waiting for rust": start rust first and wait for the main menu.
  2. "key locked to another pc": reset the hwid in my keys, or ask support.
  3. status says "updating": rust patched. wait for the status board to go green.
  4. anything else: open a ticket on discord with a screenshot of the loader.
